Quaternion conventions / the precise answer

There are two different “twos.”

The topology explains why two quaternion values can encode one attitude. It does not explain why two software systems can disagree about four numbers.
1

Representation ambiguity

SU(2) → SO(3)  is 2-to-1
R(q) = R(−q)

q and −q are antipodes on S³ that produce the same 3D rotation. A 360° path reaches −q; 720° returns to q.

Engineering consequence: attitude error, interpolation, and time histories must be sign invariant / hemisphere aligned.
