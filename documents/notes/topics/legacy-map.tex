\topic{legacy-map}{Earlier manuscripts and the growing master}
This master is an extensible synthesis, with three initial worked studies.
It does not claim to have reviewed or incorporated every historical page.
The Reading Room links the original manuscripts directly and marks their
availability separately from verification. Original files and attribution are
preserved; corrections belong in reviewed topic chapters with provenance.

\begin{description}
\item[Propulsion:] Ernest's \path{LaTeXandpdfs/Propulsion.tex} develops rocket
and nozzle physics, thermochemistry, droplets, RK order conditions, adaptive
control and CFD. It is the source map for future propulsion and numerical chapters.
\item[Continuum mechanics:] \path{LaTeXandpdfs/FluidMechanics.tex} bridges
geometric differential operators and engineering transport. In CompPhys,
\path{LaTeXandpdfs/CompPhys.tex} continues into finite volumes, staggered grids,
pressure Poisson equations and CUDA computation.
\item[Geometry and mechanics:] mathphysics's \path{LaTeX_and_pdfs/DGDT_dump.tex}
and modular \path{LeeJM/} notes supply Lie groups, connections and symplectic
geometry. Some DGDT rigid-body passages are unfinished; linking them is not
a correctness certificate.
\item[Rotations and gravitation:] \path{LaTeXandpdfs/SO3_SU2_Quaternions.tex}
and \path{documents/StormerRule.tex} contain substantial existing derivations.
Monoclaw's \path{Python/SageMath/GravitationalPotential/} has related symbolic
and numerical scripts, which require their own rerun and convention audit.
\end{description}

As material is reviewed, shared topic files enter the relevant book companion
and this thematic master without duplicating their mathematical source.
Further foundation, orbital, continuum, thermodynamic and combustion chapters
can follow the same explicit assumptions--derivation--computation--evidence path.
